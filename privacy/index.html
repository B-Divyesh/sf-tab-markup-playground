PRIVACY

Privacy for your guitar exercises

Effective September 6, 2026. Tab Playbook works without an account, uploads, or tracking scripts.

What stays in your browser

Your latest exercise is saved in browser storage so it remains when you return. Clear removes it.

How share links work

Share links encode exercise text after # in the URL. Browsers do not send that fragment in web requests. Anyone with the full link can read its exercise.

Network and advertising

The app has no ads, behavioral analytics, third-party fonts, or third-party scripts.

Your choices

You can use the app offline after it loads once. Do not put private information in a share link.
