PLAIN-LANGUAGE POLICY

Privacy

Effective August 27, 2026. Tab Playbook is designed to work without an account, server database, or tracking scripts.

What stays on your device

Your latest exercise is saved in your browser’s local storage so it is still there when you return. You can remove it with the Clear control, by clearing site data, or by using private browsing.

Share links

When you copy a share link, the exercise text is encoded in the URL fragment after #. Browsers do not send URL fragments to our server. Anyone you give the full link to can read the exercise, so do not put private information in it.

Network and analytics

The app has no advertising, behavioral analytics, third-party fonts, or third-party scripts. Normal hosting logs may temporarily record technical request information such as an IP address and browser type for reliability and abuse prevention.

Your choices

You can use the app offline after it has loaded once. Contact the site operator through sociobot.in with privacy questions.
